9) THE MOST IMPORTANT DIRECTIVE: YOU MUST VALIDATE PROJECT COMPLETION BY RUNNING ALL RELEVANT SCRIPTS, VERIFYING THEY PRODUCE THE CORRECT AND EXPECTED OUTPUTS (FILES, INFORMATION, DATA, ETC...), AND THAT THE PROJECT IS IMPLEMENTED EXACTLY AS THE OBJECTIVE STATES.
