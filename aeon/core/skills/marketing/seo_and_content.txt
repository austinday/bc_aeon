# MARKETING SKILL: SEO & content marketing
# Use when the goal is ORGANIC search visibility and content that ranks and converts — keyword
# strategy, on-page/technical SEO, and content creation. Pairs with web_dev/website_build_deploy
# (technical foundation) and marketing/marketing_campaign (strategy). Goal: pages that rank for
# the RIGHT queries and turn visitors into results.

1. KEYWORD & INTENT RESEARCH (target intent, not just volume):
- Find the terms the audience actually searches AND the intent behind each: informational ("how to X"), commercial ("best X"), transactional ("buy X"). Match content type to intent. Long-tail, lower-competition terms convert better and rank faster than head terms. Use search_web to inspect the real SERP and what the current top results cover.

2. ON-PAGE SEO (per page, one topic each):
- Primary keyword in the `<title>` (~<=60 chars), the single `<h1>`, the URL slug, and the first paragraph — naturally, not stuffed. A compelling meta description (~150 chars) — it drives click-through, not ranking. Semantic heading hierarchy, descriptive image alt text, internal links to related pages, and structured data (schema.org JSON-LD) where it fits (articles, products, FAQ).

3. TECHNICAL SEO (the foundation crawlers need):
- Crawlable and indexable: valid robots.txt, an XML sitemap. Fast (Core Web Vitals), mobile-friendly, HTTPS. Canonical tags to prevent duplicate-content dilution. No broken links / long redirect chains. CRITICAL: a client-rendered SPA serves crawlers an empty page — use SSR/prerendering/static export (see web_dev/website_build_deploy) or it won't rank at all.

4. CONTENT THAT DESERVES TO RANK:
- Genuinely answer the query BETTER than the current top results — more depth, clarity, freshness, and first-hand expertise (E-E-A-T signals). Structure for skimming: descriptive headings, lists, a short TL;DR/answer up top. Write for humans first; search engines reward content that satisfies the searcher.

5. OFF-PAGE:
- Earn links by creating genuinely useful/linkable content. AVOID link schemes, paid links, and keyword-stuffed junk — these get penalized, not rewarded.

6. MEASURE & ITERATE:
- Track rankings, organic traffic, and — most importantly — conversions from organic. Improve/refresh pages that plateau. SEO COMPOUNDS SLOWLY (weeks to months); set that expectation and don't expect overnight results.

COMMON MISTAKES:
- Keyword stuffing; writing for bots instead of humans.
- Ignoring search INTENT (ranking an informational page for a buying query, or vice versa).
- Thin/duplicate content with no canonical; no internal linking.
- A JavaScript SPA with no prerender -> not indexable.
- Chasing search volume over intent/conversion; black-hat link tactics; expecting instant ranking.
