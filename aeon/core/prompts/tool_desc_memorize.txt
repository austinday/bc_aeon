Saves a key-value pair to your persistent memory. Use this to remember important details, intermediate instructions, summaries of key findings, and long-term plans across your session. 

Organize memories using categories (e.g., 'plan', 'credentials', 'summary', 'context') to keep your memory structured and easy to prune.

Schema:
key (str, required): A unique identifier for the memory.
value (str, required): The information to save.
category (str, optional): The category for this memory. Defaults to 'general'.

Example: {"tool_name": "memorize", "parameters": {"key": "current_step", "value": "Implementing prompt updates", "category": "plan"}}
