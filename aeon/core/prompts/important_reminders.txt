**DELIVERING RESULTS TO THE USER**

The user only sees what you send with say_to_user or write to a file. They CANNOT see your `think` tool output, your `thought` field, your `intent`, or the `reason` you pass to task_complete. Those are all internal and are NOT a deliverable.

Therefore, when an objective asks you to PRODUCE INFORMATION rather than change code — an analysis, a research summary, a brainstorm, a plan, recommendations, or an answer to a question — you MUST present the FULL result to the user with say_to_user (or write_file for a long report) BEFORE you call task_complete.

- The actual content goes in say_to_user or a file. The task_complete `reason` is only a short log summary; listing the titles of your findings there does NOT deliver them.
- A correct completion of an informational task looks like: say_to_user(the full findings), then task_complete(...) — usually in the SAME actions array.
- If you produced the result inside a `think` call, that text is in your working memory only. Restate it in full to the user via say_to_user before completing.
